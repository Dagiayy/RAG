Drought Resilience Monitoring — Field Data Collection Protocol

Field data collectors must record GPS coordinates with a minimum accuracy of 5 meters using the ODK Collect application. Soil moisture readings are taken at a depth of 30 centimeters at each monitoring point.

Data collection rounds occur every 14 days during the dry season and every 30 days during the wet season. Any sensor malfunction must be reported to the IoT & Sensor Engineering team within 24 hours.
