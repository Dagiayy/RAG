Meridian Field Data & Monitoring Services — Remote Work Policy

Employees in non-field roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval. Field Operations and IoT & Sensor Engineering staff are excluded from this policy due to onsite equipment and site-visit requirements.

Remote work requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance. Employees working remotely must remain reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 local time.
